# Original Image Set

All five images were created with the built-in image generation tool in `photorealistic-natural` mode. The final WebP files are stored in `public/images/`.

## Homepage Hero

File: `public/images/valet-hero-philadelphia.webp`

Prompt: A candid 35mm editorial photograph of a diverse adult valet attendant in a deep navy uniform receiving keys beside a dark sedan at a limestone Philadelphia venue. Direct late-afternoon sunlight, hard shadows, real curb and pavement texture, rowhouses in the distance, one traffic cone, and no branding, readable plates, text, or stock-photo polish.

## Event Team

File: `public/images/event-valet-team.webp`

Prompt: Three diverse adult valet attendants in matching navy uniforms actively manage arrivals at a historic brick Philadelphia event venue. One checks the arrival list, one opens a passenger door, and one directs traffic. Crisp morning sunlight, autumn leaves, key hooks, cones, ordinary pavement, layered documentary action, and no staged group pose or visible branding.

## Team Briefing

File: `public/images/valet-team-briefing.webp`

Prompt: Four diverse adult valet attendants in consistent navy uniforms hold a candid pre-shift briefing around a curb plan, radios, and a practical key cabinet in a Philadelphia loading area. Strong side sunlight, brick and limestone texture, no one looking at camera, and no corporate headshot pose, readable documents, or visible branding.

## Shared Constraints

- Professional deep navy uniform, white shirt, dark tie, black trousers, and a small plain gold lapel pin
- Photorealistic editorial service photography with subtle grain
- Real pavement, curbs, traffic-control objects, and operational detail
- Mostly direct light with a mixture of sun, shade, and warm artificial light
- Adult subjects only
- No visible logos, readable license plates, watermark, or in-image text
- No limousine imagery, showroom surfaces, or exaggerated luxury
